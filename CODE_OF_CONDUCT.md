# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free research and software
community. Discuss evidence and implementation choices directly, without personal
attacks, discrimination, intimidation or disclosure of private information.

Scientific disagreements should identify the claim, evidence, test or falsifier
at issue. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, privacy-violating or
off-topic contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Until a public support address is chosen, conduct reports should be sent privately
to the project owner rather than posted with sensitive details. The release
metadata must name a confidential contact before public launch.
